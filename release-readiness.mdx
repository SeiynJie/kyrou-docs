This guide is for a Philippine-based founder launching a paid, local-first desktop product through a website. It assumes Google OAuth accounts, local planner content, and account/payment/support data handled by Kyrou or vendors.

<Warning>
This is a planning guide, not legal or tax advice. Have a Philippine lawyer and accountant review the final documents before accepting payment.
</Warning>

## Before accepting customers

Prepare Terms of Service, a desktop EULA, Privacy Notice, pricing/subscription/refund policy, Acceptable Use Policy, support and complaint process, security contact, cookie notice where needed, third-party/open-source notices, update policy, vendor register, IP ownership file, contributor assignments, retention/deletion workflow, and breach-response runbook.

Google OAuth still processes personal data even when task content stays on-device. See the [Philippine Data Privacy Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/) and [NPC consent guidance](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/NPC-Circular-No.-2023-04_Guidelines-on-Consent_07Nov2023.pdf).

## Philippine setup

| Route | Advantage | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual | Fastest validation | You personally carry contracts, tax, debts, and liability |
| Sole proprietorship | Simple owner-operated business | Not a separate legal person |
| OPC | Clearer separation and ownership | SEC, tax, accounting, and reporting obligations |
| Staged | Launch first, incorporate later | Later assignment of IP, contracts, accounts, and vendors |

For an OPC, prepare SEC name reservation, Articles of Incorporation, nominee details, officers, resolutions, corporate accounts, IP assignment, BIR registration, invoicing, and applicable LGU permits. See the [SEC Corporation Code](https://www.sec.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/2021RCC_REVISED-CORPORATION-CODE_as-of-June-102021.pdf), [SEC reporting requirements](https://www.sec.gov.ph/reportorial-requirements/corporations-with-primary-licenses/), and [SEC eSPARC](https://learn-esparc.sec.gov.ph/application/select-regular). For taxes, use [BIR NewBizReg](https://web-services.bir.gov.ph/newbizreg/) and have an accountant set the filing calendar.

## Protecting “Kyrou”

Copyright protects code, artwork, copy, and other expression—not the product name. Search similar marks, secure the domain and social handles, preserve dated first-use evidence, file the word mark through IPOPHL, and file the logo separately if valuable. Consider copyright deposit for important code or creative works and maintain signed contributor assignments.

Use [IPOPHL trademark filing](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/trademark/filing/) and [IPOPHL copyright](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/copyright/) as the official starting points. Consider foreign filings only after selecting priority markets.

## Payments and privacy

Document pricing, renewals, cancellation, trials, refunds, failed payments, chargebacks, digital delivery, invoices, taxes/VAT, and payment-provider disputes. Keep a data inventory that separates local task content from OAuth identity, payment, support, analytics, crash, and future sync data. Add a retention schedule, deletion/export process, data-subject request workflow, vendor list, access controls, secret management, backups, and breach response.

## Future mobile release

Before Apple or Google distribution, add developer and billing agreements, store privacy labels/data-safety declarations, account deletion rules, in-app subscription terms, signing records, crash-report disclosures, and store refund handling. These are intentionally deferred until mobile distribution is active.

## Order of work

1. Clear the name and secure domains/handles.
2. Choose an individual/sole-proprietor launch or OPC.
3. Inventory IP and sign assignments.
4. Complete applicable SEC/DTI/BIR/LGU work.
5. Finalize Terms, Privacy Notice, EULA, refunds, support, and security contact.
6. Review OAuth and payment-provider agreements.
7. File the Philippine trademark.
8. Obtain legal/accounting review before accepting payment.
9. Add country and mobile-store annexes when those channels become active.
