Kyrou (formerly Kairo) is a local-first, mobile-first daily planner. A calm, high-contrast planner for Today, Week, and Calendar views — all your data stays on your device.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Today & Calendar" icon="calendar-days">
